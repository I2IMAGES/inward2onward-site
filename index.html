AI agents

The AI agents we build

Customer agents

They answer customer questions and resolve common issues, day and night. They work on your website, by phone, by email and by text.

Employee agents

They take repeat tasks off your team. They also track tasks and move work from one step to the next.

Creative agents

They draft content and support design work in your brand’s style.

Data agents

They analyze your data and report what it shows, to support your decisions.

Code agents

They write and test code to the standards you set.

Security agents

They watch for threats around the clock and flag them to you.

How we work

How a build runs

  1. 1

    Discovery

    We map how your work runs today. We pick the tasks an agent will handle and agree with you on how to measure success.

  2. 2

    Design

    We plan how the agent works and how it connects to your systems. We set up tests for the agent.

  3. 3

    Deploy

    We set up the agent, connect it and test it. Then we train your team and hand it over.

  4. 4

    Optimize

    We track how the agent performs and tune it as it runs.

FAQ

Questions about working with us

Do I need technical skills to work with you?

No. In AI coaching, you build your own AI assistant in Claude with EJ Steele, one part of your business at a time. In a build, we set up the system for you.

What happens after you build a system?

We test it, train the people who will run it and hand it over. The system is yours to run.

What is the difference between AI coaching and a build?

In AI coaching, you build and use your own AI assistant, with EJ Steele in each session. In a build, we set up the system for you, then hand it over.

Which tools do you work with?

We build CRMs in Keap and GoHighLevel and automate tasks with Make.com, n8n and MindStudio. We also work with Claude, ChatGPT and the other tools listed on this page.

What is the first call?

A 30-minute online call with EJ Steele. You can book it from any Book a call button on this page.

Where are you based?

Glendale, Arizona. We serve clients nationally.
